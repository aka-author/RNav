Integrating the Plug-In into a DITA OT Instance

The following prerequisites are mandatory for deploying the plug-in on your computer.

  • The plug-in is installed into your DITA OT folder.

Integrate the plug-in into your DITA OT instance after you have deployed it. Otherwise the DITA OT will be unable to apply the plug-in to your DITA projects.

1.
Turn-on a command-line mode.
2.
Make the folder C:\DITAOT current.
3.
Run an integration command.
bin\ant -f integrator.xml

An expected system output is listed below. 

Unable to locate tools.jar. Expected to find it in C:\Program Files (x86)\Java\jre-1.8\lib\tools.jar
Buildfile: C:\DITAOT\integrator.xml
                    
strict:
                    
integrate:
      [jar] Building jar: C:\DITAOT\lib\dost-configuration.jar
                    
BUILD SUCCESSFUL
Total time: 1 second

The plug-in is integrated into your instance of the DITA OT now.
