Deploying the Plug-in on Your Computer

The following prerequisites are mandatory for deploying the plug-in on your computer.

  • The DITA Open Toolkit (DITA OT) is deployed on your computer. We assume here that a target DITA OT instance is available in a folder C:\DITAOT.
  • No previous version of the plug-in is installed in the DITA OT instance.
  • An archive com.ditatoo.weboutput is available somewhere on your computer.
1.
Extract files from the archive into a temporary folder.

A folder com.ditatoo.weboutput appears in the temporary folder.

2.
Copy the folder com.ditatoo.weboutput into the folder DITAOT\plugins.

The plug-in is installed into your instance of the DITA OT now. You are ready to use the plug-in with the DITA OT, but not with DITAToo so far.

Integrate the plug-in into your instance of DITA OT.
