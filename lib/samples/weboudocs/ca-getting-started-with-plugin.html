Getting Started with the Plug-In

Embark on your plug-in journey by following these essential steps to get started:

  1. Deploying the plug-in on your computer
  2. Integrating the plug-in into your DITA OT instance
  3. Integrating the plug-in into your DITAToo instance
  4. Customizing your instance of the plug-in

While the first three steps are straightforward and easy to accomplish, the final step may require some additional effort, especially if the layout you plan to implement is complex in nature.
