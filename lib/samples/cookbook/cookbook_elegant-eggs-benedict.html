Elegant Eggs Benedict

Ingredients:

  • 4 English muffins, split and toasted
  • 8 slices of Canadian bacon or ham
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 3 large egg yolks
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • Pinch of cayenne pepper (optional)
  • Fresh chives or parsley for garnish (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Prepare the hollandaise sauce: In a blender, combine egg yolks, lemon juice, and salt. Blend on low speed for a few seconds.
  2. With the blender running, slowly drizzle in the melted butter until the sauce thickens. Add a pinch of cayenne pepper if desired. Set aside and keep warm.
  3. In a skillet, heat the Canadian bacon or ham over medium heat until lightly browned. Remove and set aside.
  4. Bring a medium saucepan of water to a gentle simmer. Add white vinegar to the water.
  5. Crack each egg into a small cup or bowl. Carefully slide the eggs, one at a time, into the simmering water. Cook for 3-4 minutes, or until the whites are set but the yolks remain runny. Remove with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels.
  6. Assemble the Eggs Benedict: Place a slice of toasted English muffin on a plate, followed by a slice of Canadian bacon or ham. Top with a poached egg.
  7. Spoon a generous amount of hollandaise sauce over the top. Repeat for all servings.
  8. Garnish with fresh chives or parsley, if desired, and serve immediately.

Note: Eggs Benedict is perfect for a ceremonial breakfast or brunch. For variety, try using smoked salmon or sauteed spinach instead of Canadian bacon. Pair it with fresh fruit, champagne, or orange juice for a complete elegant meal.
