Meat Pie

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb ground beef or lamb
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn)
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 1/2 cups beef or chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 sheet puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a large skillet over medium heat, cook the ground beef or lamb, onion, and garlic until the meat is browned and cooked through. Drain any excess fat.
  3. Stir in the frozen vegetables, flour, broth, cream, salt, and pepper. Bring the mixture to a simmer and cook for a few minutes until thickened.
  4. Roll out the puff pastry sheet on a lightly floured surface to a thickness of about 1/8 inch.
  5. Transfer the meat mixture to a 9-inch pie dish. Place the pastry sheet over the top of the meat mixture, tucking the edges of the pastry down into the sides of the dish. Brush the beaten egg over the pastry.
  6. Bake the pie for 25-30 minutes, or until the pastry is puffed and golden brown.
  7. Let the pie cool for a few minutes before serving.

Note: You can use any ground meat of your choice, such as pork, turkey or chicken. You can also add different spices and herbs to the meat mixture to customize the flavor. You can use store-bought puff pastry or make your own. If you want to make your own, Here is a recipe for puff pastry dough.

  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, chilled and cut into small pieces
  • 1/2 cup ice water

Instructions for puff pastry dough:

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour and salt. Using a pastry cutter or your fingers, cut the butter into the flour mixture until it resembles coarse crumbs.
  2. Gradually add the ice water, stirring with a fork, until the dough comes together and forms a ball.
  3. Flatten the dough into a disk and wrap it in plastic wrap. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before using.
