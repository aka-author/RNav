Salad Caesar

Ingredients:

  • 1 head of romaine lettuce, washed and dried
  • 1/2 cup croutons
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Instructions:

  1. Cut the romaine lettuce into bite-sized pieces and place in a large mixing bowl.
  2. In a separate small mixing bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, lemon juice, garlic, salt, and pepper.
  3. Slowly drizzle in the olive oil while whisking continuously to emulsify the dressing.
  4. Add the croutons and grated Parmesan cheese to the lettuce and toss to combine.
  5. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss again to evenly coat the lettuce.
  6. Serve immediately and enjoy!

Note: You can add different ingredients to customize the flavor of the Caesar salad, such as diced grilled chicken, shrimp, or bacon. You can also add different types of cheese, such as blue cheese or feta.

It is important to use fresh and crispy romaine lettuce. It's also worth noting that raw eggs are used in the original recipe for Caesar salad dressing, but in this recipe, we use mayonnaise that already contains eggs, so it is safe to consume.

Caesar salad is a classic salad that is perfect for a light lunch or as a side dish. It's easy to make and it can be customized to suit your taste preferences.
