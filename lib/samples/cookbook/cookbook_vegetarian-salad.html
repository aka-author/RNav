Vegetarian Salad

Ingredients:

  • 4 cups mixed greens (spinach, arugula, lettuce, etc)
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cup diced cucumber
  • 1/4 cup diced red onion
  • 1/4 cup crumbled feta cheese (optional)
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh basil or parsley (optional)

For the dressing:

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Instructions:

  1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the mixed greens, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, and red onion.
  2. In a small mixing bowl, whisk together the olive oil, red wine vinegar, Dijon mustard, salt, and pepper.
  3. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss to coat.
  4. Add the feta cheese and chopped basil or parsley (if using) and toss again.
  5. Serve immediately and enjoy!

Note: You can add different ingredients to customize the flavor of the salad, such as diced avocado, roasted bell peppers, pickled onions, or sliced mushrooms. You can also add different types of cheese, such as goat cheese or blue cheese. You can also add different types of nuts, such as almonds, walnuts, or pecans to add some crunch.

You can also add different types of greens, such as kale, romaine, or endive, to give a variety of texture and taste. You can also add different types of beans, such as chickpeas, lentils, or black beans. You can also use different types of dressings, such as balsamic vinaigrette, ranch or lemon vinaigrette.

This salad is a great way to incorporate a variety of vegetables and flavors into your diet, and it's easy to customize to your taste preferences.
