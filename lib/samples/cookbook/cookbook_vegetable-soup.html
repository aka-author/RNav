Vegetable Soup

Ingredients:

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 2 cups diced potatoes
  • 2 cups diced carrots
  • 2 cups diced celery
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes
  • 6 cups vegetable broth or water
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 tsp dried basil (optional)
  • 1 tsp dried thyme (optional)
  • 1 bay leaf (optional)
  • 1 cup chopped kale or spinach (optional)

Instructions:

  1. In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the onion and garlic and cook until softened.
  2. Add the potatoes, carrots, celery, and tomatoes to the pot. Pour in the vegetable broth or water and stir.
  3. Add the basil, thyme, bay leaf (if using) and bring the soup to a boil.
  4. Reduce the heat to low and let the soup simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until the vegetables are tender.
  5. Add the chopped kale or spinach (if using) and stir until wilted.
  6. Season the soup with salt and pepper to taste. Remove bay leaf before serving.

Note: You can use any vegetables of your choice, such as squash, green beans, or corn. You can also add different spices and herbs to customize the flavor of the soup. You can also use chicken or beef broth instead of water for added flavor.
