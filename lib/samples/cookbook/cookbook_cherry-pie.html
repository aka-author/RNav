Cherry Pie

Ingredients:

  • 1 recipe for double crust pie dough (for the crust and top)
  • 4 cups fresh or frozen cherries, pitted and stemmed
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon unsalted butter, cut into small pieces
  • 1 egg, beaten (for egg wash)
  • Coarse sugar, for sprinkling (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. On a lightly floured surface, roll out half of the pie dough to fit a 9-inch pie dish. Gently press the dough into the bottom and sides of the dish.
  3. In a large mixing bowl, combine the sugar, cornstarch, cinnamon (if using), and lemon juice. Add the cherries and toss to coat. Pour the cherry mixture into the prepared crust. Dot the top with butter.
  4. Roll out the remaining dough and place it on top of the cherries. Trim the edges and crimp to seal the crust. Brush the top with beaten egg and sprinkle with coarse sugar, if desired.
  5. Place the pie on a baking sheet and bake for about 60-70 minutes or until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbling.
  6. Let the pie cool for at least 2 hours before serving to allow the filling to set.

Note: You can use fresh or frozen cherries for the pie, but if using frozen make sure they are thawed and drained before using them. You can use store-bought pie dough or make your own. If you want to make your own, here is a recipe for double crust pie dough:

  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, chilled and cut into small pieces
  • 1/2 cup ice water

Instructions for double crust pie dough:

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour and salt. Using a pastry cutter or your fingers, cut the butter into the flour mixture until it resembles coarse crumbs.
  2. Gradually add the ice water, stirring with a fork, until the dough comes together and forms a ball.
  3. Divide the dough into two and shape each half into a disk. Wrap each disk in plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before using.
