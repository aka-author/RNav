Hamburger

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb ground beef (80% lean)
  • Salt, to taste
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • Optional toppings:
    • Lettuce leaves
    • Sliced tomato
    • Sliced pickles
    • Sliced onion
    • Cheese slices
    • Ketchup, mustard, or mayonnaise

Instructions:

  1. Preheat a grill, grill pan, or non-stick skillet to medium-high heat.
  2. Divide the ground beef into four equal portions and gently shape them into patties about 3/4 inch thick.
  3. Season both sides of each patty with salt and freshly ground black pepper.
  4. Cook the patties on the preheated grill or pan for 3-4 minutes per side for medium doneness, or adjust the cooking time to your preference.
  5. If adding cheese, place a slice on each patty during the last minute of cooking to allow it to melt.
  6. Toast the hamburger buns lightly on the grill or in the pan if desired.
  7. Assemble the hamburgers by placing each patty on the bottom half of a bun, and add your preferred toppings and condiments.
  8. Place the top half of the bun over the toppings and serve immediately.

Note: You can customize your hamburger with a wide range of toppings and sauces to suit your taste. For a healthier option, consider using leaner ground beef or turkey.
