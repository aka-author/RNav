Octopus Soup

Octopus soup is a traditional dish that is made using octopus meat and a variety of vegetables. Here is a basic recipe for an octopus soup:

Ingredients:

  • 2 lb octopus, cleaned and cut into small pieces
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 onions, diced
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 2 cups diced potatoes
  • 2 cups diced carrots
  • 2 cups diced celery
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes
  • 6 cups chicken or fish broth
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 tsp dried basil (optional)
  • 1 tsp dried thyme (optional)
  • 1 bay leaf (optional)
  • 1 cup chopped kale or spinach (optional)

Instructions:

  1. In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the octopus and cook for about 5 minutes or until it starts to turn opaque. Remove from the pot and set aside.
  2. Add the onions and garlic and cook until softened.
  3. Add the potatoes, carrots, celery, and tomatoes to the pot. Pour in the chicken or fish broth and stir.
  4. Add the basil, thyme, bay leaf (if using) and bring the soup to a boil.
  5. Reduce the heat to low and let the soup simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until the vegetables are tender.
  6. Add the cooked octopus back to the pot and continue to simmer for an additional 5 minutes.
  7. Add the chopped kale or spinach (if using) and stir until wilted.
  8. Season the soup with salt and pepper to taste. Remove bay leaf before serving.

Note: Cooking octopus properly is important, if it's not cooked enough, it can be tough and rubbery. To make sure the octopus is tender, simmer it in water for about 1 hour before proceeding with the recipe. You can also add different vegetables of your choice, such as squash, green beans, or corn. You can also add different spices and herbs to customize the flavor of the soup.

It's worth noting that octopus is considered a delicacy, and it is not always readily available in all areas. If you can't find octopus, you can also make a similar soup using squid or other seafood.
