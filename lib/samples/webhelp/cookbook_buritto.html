Breakfast Burrito

Ingredients:

  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 6 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheese (cheddar or Monterey Jack)
  • 1/2 cup cooked and crumbled sausage or bacon (optional)
  • 1/2 cup diced vegetables (bell peppers, onions, or tomatoes)
  • 2 tbsp butter or oil
  • Salsa or hot sauce (optional)

Instructions:

  1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, salt, and pepper.
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium heat and melt the butter or heat the oil.
  3. Add the whisked eggs to the skillet and cook, stirring frequently, until scrambled and fully cooked. Remove from heat.
  4. In the same skillet, saute the diced vegetables for 2-3 minutes until softened. Remove from heat.
  5. Warm the flour tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave for a few seconds until pliable.
  6. Assemble each burrito by layering scrambled eggs, sauteed vegetables, shredded cheese, and cooked sausage or bacon in the center of the tortilla.
  7. Fold in the sides of the tortilla and roll it tightly into a burrito shape.
  8. If desired, lightly toast each burrito in a skillet until golden brown on the outside.
  9. Serve immediately with salsa or hot sauce on the side.

Note: You can customize your breakfast burrito with additional ingredients, such as avocado, spinach, or beans, to suit your taste. To save time, prepare the filling ingredients ahead of time and reheat before assembling.
