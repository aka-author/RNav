Fried Eggs

Ingredients:

  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tbsp butter or 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • Salt, to taste
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Instructions:

  1. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and add the butter or oil.
  2. Once the butter is melted and sizzling (or the oil is shimmering), crack the eggs into the skillet, being careful not to break the yolks.
  3. Reduce the heat to medium-low and cook the eggs to your preferred doneness:
    1. For sunny-side up: Cook without flipping until the whites are set and the yolks are still runny (about 2-3 minutes).
    2. For over-easy: Cook for 2-3 minutes, then gently flip the eggs and cook for an additional 30 seconds to 1 minute.
    3. For over-hard: Cook for 2-3 minutes, flip, and cook until the yolks are fully cooked through (about 1-2 minutes).
  4. Season with salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste.
  5. Carefully transfer the eggs to a plate and serve immediately.

Note: For extra flavor, consider adding toppings such as chopped herbs, grated cheese, or a drizzle of hot sauce.
